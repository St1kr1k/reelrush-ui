What's in the pack

Screens

  • HUD: currencies, menu, quest tracker, promos, hotbar
  • Shop with three tabs and rarity cards
  • Inventory with detail panel, favorite, sell, sell all
  • Rebirth, Daily Rewards, Fish Index, Settings
  • Confirm popup and stacking toasts

Animation

  • Windows pop in with a back-ease and background blur
  • Buttons scale on hover and squash on press
  • Counters tick up, progress bars fill on open
  • Toggles slide, cards pop on purchase or claim

Built for editing

  • Real Frames, UICorner, UIStroke, UIGradient, layouts
  • One 1280×720 design canvas scaled to every screen size
  • Items, prices and rarities stored as Values on each card
  • Logic in one commented LocalScript, wired by names

Delivered as a Studio builder: one command creates the whole ScreenGui in StarterGui.
